AdDash

AdDash is an Android app that shows your Google AdMob earnings, such as revenue, impressions, clicks and eCPM, for one or more of your own AdMob accounts, in the app and in home-screen widgets.

You sign in with your Google account and allow read-only access to your AdMob reports. Your data is fetched straight from Google to your phone and stays on your phone.
