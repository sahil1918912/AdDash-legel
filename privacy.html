AdDash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

AdDash ("the app", package com.cleandesign.addash) is developed by Clean Design ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly, the app reads your AdMob account details and earnings reports (for example revenue, impressions, clicks, ad requests, and breakdowns by app, country, ad format and ad unit) using the Google AdMob API.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to show you your own earnings in the app and its widgets.
  • It is requested directly from Google's servers by your phone and is stored only on your device.
  • I do not receive, collect, store or have access to your AdMob data or your Google account data. It is not sent to any server operated by me.
  • I do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than showing it to you.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AdDash's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Diagnostics and analytics

The app uses Google Firebase services: Firebase Analytics (basic app usage events), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports with device model, Android version and app version) and Cloud Firestore (to read non-personal app configuration). These do not include your AdMob earnings. They are processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.

Retention and deleting your data

Data stored on your phone is removed when you sign out in the app or uninstall it. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
